Monthly Bingo at Downsville Ruritan Club

Dates & Times

When: 2nd Friday of every month, September through May

Doors open: 4:30 PM
Early birds start: 5:30 PM
Regular bingo starts: 6:00 PM

Description

Come enjoy an evening of community bingo with great food, tip jars, and amazing raffle prizes! This is a family-friendly event held at the Downsville Ruritan Club in Williamsport, Maryland.

Admission: $20 includes 20 regular bingo games
Extra tickets and coveralls: Sold separately
Note: No outside food permitted. Must be 18 or older to claim tip jars.
