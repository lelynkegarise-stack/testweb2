Our Story

History of the Downsville Ruritan Club

Founded in 1954, the Downsville Ruritan Club has been building stronger local connections for generations.

Founded in 1954

The Downsville Ruritan Club was founded in 1954 as part of the national Ruritan network, a civic organization dedicated to community service, fellowship, and goodwill. For over 70 years, we have worked to improve the lives of people in Downsville, Williamsport, and surrounding communities.

Our Journey

Over the decades, the Downsville Ruritan Club has grown from a small group of dedicated community volunteers into an organization that touches many lives each year through our events, fundraising, service projects, and community outreach.

What started in 1954 as a vision to “create a better understanding among people and improve America’s communities” has become a reality through the tireless efforts of our members and volunteers.

Community Impact

Volunteer Support

We provide hands-on help for local organizations, projects, and community programs that benefit families and residents.

Local Events

Our events bring neighbors together for fellowship, fundraising, fun, and meaningful local connection.

Long-Term Legacy

Generations of community members have stepped up to serve, making the club a strong local institution.

  • Provided facilities for youth organizations and community groups
  • Hosted annual events that bring the community together
  • Supported local families and individuals in times of need
  • Invested in community infrastructure and public spaces
  • Fostered fellowship and goodwill among neighbors
  • Mentored young people through youth programs and support

Looking Forward

As we look to the future, the Downsville Ruritan Club remains committed to our founding principles of fellowship, goodwill, and community service. We continue to adapt to meet the evolving needs of our area while keeping our mission strong and rooted in local service.
