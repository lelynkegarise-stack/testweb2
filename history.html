Downsville Ruritan Club History

Founded in 1954

The Downsville Ruritan Club was founded in 1954 as part of the national Ruritan network, a civic organization dedicated to community service, fellowship, and goodwill. For over 70 years, we have been a cornerstone of community life in Downsville and the surrounding Williamsport, Maryland area.

Our Journey

Over the decades, the Downsville Ruritan Club has grown from a small group of dedicated community volunteers into an organization that touches thousands of lives each year through our events, programs, and community service initiatives.

What started in 1954 as a vision to "create a better understanding among people and improve America's communities" has become a reality through the tireless efforts of our members and volunteers.

Community Impact

Throughout our history, the Downsville Ruritan Club has:

  • Provided facilities for youth organizations and community groups
  • Hosted annual events that bring the community together
  • Supported local families and individuals in times of need
  • Invested in community infrastructure and public spaces
  • Fostered fellowship and goodwill among neighbors
  • Mentored young people through youth programs and support

Looking Forward

As we look to the future, the Downsville Ruritan Club remains committed to our founding principles of fellowship, goodwill, and community service. We continue to adapt to meet the evolving needs of Downsville and surrounding communities while maintaining the values that have guided us since 1954.
