Rental Agreement & Regulations

Note: Rentals will not be reserved until the full balance is received. Cancellations must be made 2 weeks prior to the reservation date to receive a refund.

  1. No alcoholic beverages permitted on the property.
  2. No tape, nails, or tacks on any walls, ceilings, windows, or floors.
  3. Rental time is strictly from 9:00 AM until 10:30 PM. Failure to leave by 10:30 PM will result in forfeiture of the security deposit.
  4. Adult supervision is required at all times.
  5. Premises are monitored by 24/7 security cameras.

Cleanup Guidelines

Mops and brooms are available in the storage closet for your convenience:

  • All spills must be cleaned up immediately.
  • Tables and chairs must be wiped clean and returned to their original positions.
  • All kitchen equipment must be cleaned and turned off. Secure the refrigerator door and remove all food/beverages.
  • All trash must be placed in the outside dumpster, and the dumpster area must be left clean.
