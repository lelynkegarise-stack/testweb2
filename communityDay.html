Downsville Community Day

Date & Time

Saturday, October 17, 2026
9:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Description

Come enjoy a free day of vendors and activities in Downsville! This community event features:

  • Local vendors and crafts
  • Raffles with great prizes
  • Tip jar games
  • 50/50 drawings
  • Great food from local vendors
  • Free activities for kids including:
    • Face painting
    • Pumpkin painting
    • Scarecrow making
    • Cotton candy

Interested in being a vendor? Contact Chasity Long at 301-334-0427

For more information, visit our Facebook event page.
