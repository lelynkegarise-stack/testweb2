Our Purpose

Mission of the Downsville Ruritan Club

For more than 70 years, the Downsville Ruritan Club has worked to strengthen the community through service, fellowship, and goodwill.

Serving with Purpose

The Downsville Ruritan Club is dedicated to improving local communities and building a better America through fellowship, goodwill, and community service. Since 1954, we have been serving Downsville, Williamsport, and neighboring communities with practical, hands-on support.

Each Ruritan club surveys the needs of its community and works to meet those needs through volunteer efforts, local partnerships, and shared responsibility. Our members believe that stronger neighborhoods begin with caring neighbors.

What We Do in Downsville

Support Youth

We provide support for youth programs, local activities, and community organizations working to help young people thrive.

Host Events

From holiday programs to community gatherings, we create opportunities for neighbors to connect and celebrate together.

Serve Neighbors

We offer practical help through local food drives, hygiene baskets, volunteer work, and community support initiatives.

  • Supporting local Boy Scout Troop 58 with meeting and practice facilities
  • Providing resources for Conococheague Little League baseball
  • Supporting local youth football programs
  • Hosting community events including Christmas for Kids programs
  • Organizing Easter egg hunts and family activities
  • Providing hygiene baskets and assistance to those in need
  • Sponsoring the Williamsport Carnival and other community events
  • Offering affordable community space rentals for local families and organizations

Our Commitment

Our slogan is “Fellowship, Goodwill, and Community Service.” We believe in bringing people of all occupations, social positions, and backgrounds together to improve our community. People of all ages, backgrounds, and interests are welcome to join in the effort.

We are committed to making our area a better place to live, work, and raise a family—one act of service at a time.
