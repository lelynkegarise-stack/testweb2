Downsville Ruritan Club Mission

The Downsville Ruritan Club is dedicated to improving local communities and building a better America through fellowship, goodwill, and community service. Since 1954, we have been serving Downsville, Williamsport, and the surrounding Washington County area with dedication to our core mission.

Each Ruritan club surveys the needs of its community and works to meet those needs through volunteer efforts and community engagement.

What We Do in Downsville

The Downsville Ruritan works with local organizations and community groups to strengthen our area, including:

  • Supporting local Boy Scout Troop 58 with meeting and practice facilities
  • Providing resources for Conocochegue Little League baseball
  • Supporting local youth football programs
  • Hosting community events including Christmas for Kids programs
  • Organizing Easter egg hunts and family activities
  • Providing hygiene baskets and assistance to those in need
  • Sponsoring the Williamsport Carnival and other community events
  • Offering affordable community space rentals for local families and organizations

Our Commitment

Our slogan is "Fellowship, Goodwill, and Community Service." We believe in bringing people of all occupations, social positions, and backgrounds together to improve our community. People of all backgrounds are encouraged to join and become part of our mission.
